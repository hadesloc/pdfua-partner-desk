Common questions

Are client PDFs ever uploaded to public AI clouds?
No. Paid remediation batches are processed on an offline-capable Windows workstation in Vietnam with zero third-party AI model training on client files. Files uploaded to our free online veraPDF 1.30.3 checker (up to 20 MB) are inspected in an isolated temporary directory and deleted immediately upon response (0 files retained). Mutual NDAs are welcomed for agency white-label partners.
What exact files are included in each delivery?
Each order delivers an unbranded white-label ZIP archive containing: (1) your remediated PDF with 100% pixel-identical visual layout, (2) a veraPDF 1.30.3 XML/HTML report confirming 0 failed ISO 14289-1 (PDF/UA-1) rules, (3) an NVDA screen-reader reading-order QA log, and (4) a SHA-256 checksum manifest.
What are your standard turnaround times?
Complimentary 1–10 page proof samples and $19 Single-PDF Express orders (1–5 pages) are returned within 24 business hours. Larger fixed-price packages deliver in 2 business days ($65 Starter, up to 12 pages), 4 business days ($195 Reports & Tables, up to 30 pages), and 5 business days ($390 Scanned OCR up to 35p or Fillable AcroForms up to 25p). Agency wholesale batches ($2.00–$6.50/page) average 48–72 business hours.
Can you remediate scanned ordinances and fillable PDF forms?
Yes. Scanned image PDFs receive an invisible searchable Unicode text layer (PDF rendering mode 3 Tr via RapidOCR ONNX) beneath the original page image so visual appearance stays unchanged while text becomes selectable and tagged. Fillable AcroForms receive spoken /TU tooltips, <Form> /OBJR structure tags, and /Tabs /S keyboard tab order.
How do you handle cryptographically signed PDFs?
Editing a PDF tag tree changes byte offsets and invalidates existing digital signatures (/Sig /ByteRange). We flag signed files upfront at no cost so your team can supply the unsigned source PDF before final signing or authorize removing an obsolete signature wrapper.
Does PDF/UA remediation constitute legal advice?
No. We provide technical document accessibility engineering and machine/screen-reader conformance testing against ISO 14289-1:2014, WCAG 2.1 Level AA, and Revised Section 508 technical standards—not legal counsel.
