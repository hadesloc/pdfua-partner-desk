Every order includes a 14-day free redo guarantee: if a delivered PDF triggers any ISO 14289-1 failure in veraPDF 1.30 or PAC 2026, or exhibits a reading-order, table-header, alt-text, or form-tooltip defect in NVDA or JAWS, we fix and re-certify it within 24 business hours at $0 extra charge. Can you remediate scanned ordinances and interactive fillable AcroForms?

Yes. For image-only scans, we embed an invisible Unicode OCR text layer (PDF rendering mode 3 Tr via RapidOCR ONNX) beneath the original scan image so visual appearance stays 100% unchanged while text becomes selectable and tagged. For fillable AcroForms, we author human-readable /TU spoken tooltips, bind widgets via <Form> and /OBJR structure elements, and set /Tabs /S structure focus order.

How do you handle cryptographically signed PDFs?

Editing internal PDF tag trees alters byte offsets and invalidates existing cryptographic digital signatures (/Sig /ByteRange). We flag signed files upfront at $0 cost so your team can either supply the unsigned source PDF for remediation prior to final signing or authorize stripping an obsolete signature lock.
