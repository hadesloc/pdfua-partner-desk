Expanded 124-Domain Public Crawl Corpus (774 PDFs, 19,022 Pages)

Across our broader baseline crawl of 124 public-sector and higher-education domains (28 .gov, 89 .edu, and 7 public-interest portals), veraPDF flagged 771 of 774 unique PDFs (99.61%) totaling 5,453 failed rule instances and 3,891,504 failed clause checks (225/225 failing on .gov; 524/527 failing on .edu). Crucially, 76.87% (595/774) already contained export tags yet 99.50% (592/595) still failed ISO 14289-1.

Within the expanded audit cohort of 15 U.S. digital agency public-sector client portfolios totaling 5,384 pages (oakwoodohio.gov, dublinohiousa.gov, cityofseaside.us, and peers), baseline scans flagged missing /StructTreeRoot, pdfuaid:part=1 XMP identifiers, table /Scope, and subset font CIDSet metrics across every sampled backlog. All 15 representative deliverables passed veraPDF 1.30.3 with 0 failed rules and 100% pixel preservation.

Honest Machine-vs-Human Scope Notice

Passing veraPDF 1.30.3 (0 failed rules) proves ISO 14289-1 machine syntax conformance—not full human semantic usability under WCAG 2.1 AA. Every Studio QA bundle initializes OPERATOR-QA.md with human_review: PENDING so your reviewer verifies reading order, descriptive <Figure> alt text, color contrast, and NVDA/PAC 2026 playback before publishing.
