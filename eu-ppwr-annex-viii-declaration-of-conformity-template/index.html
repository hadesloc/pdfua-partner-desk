Frequently asked questions

When does Regulation (EU) 2025/40 (PPWR) require an Annex VIII EU Declaration of Conformity?
PPWR entered into force in February 2025 and applies generally from August 12, 2026, replacing Directive 94/62/EC and requiring structured Annex VII technical documentation and an Annex VIII EU Declaration of Conformity for packaging placed on the EU market.
Does PackProof sign the Declaration of Conformity as an EU Authorised Representative?
No. PackProof is pure document-compilation and threshold-checking software. Your legal manufacturer or appointed EU importer/operator reviews and signs the generated Annex VIII document under its own responsibility.
