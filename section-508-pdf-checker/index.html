Why fillable PDF forms & complex tables fail Section 508 QA

  • Untagged widget annotations: Interactive text boxes, checkboxes, and radio buttons lack <Form> structure parents and /OBJR object references.
  • Missing /TU spoken tooltips: Visual labels sit next to boxes on screen, but screen readers announce blank fields without explicit /TU descriptions.
  • Broken keyboard focus order: Pages with links or form inputs omit /Tabs /S, forcing Tab order to jump across visual columns.
  • Complex multi-column tables: Financial and fee tables omit <TH Scope="Column"> / <TH Scope="Row"> or leave stray spans directly inside <TR>.
