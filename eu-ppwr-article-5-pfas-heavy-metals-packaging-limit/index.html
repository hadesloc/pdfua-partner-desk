Exact quantitative limits under Regulation (EU) 2025/40 Article 5 (Aug 12, 2026)

Art. 5(4): <= 100 mg/kg Heavy Metals Sum

The sum of concentration levels of Lead (Pb), Cadmium (Cd), Mercury (Hg), and Hexavalent Chromium (Cr(VI)) in packaging or homogeneous packaging components must not exceed 100 mg/kg (ppm).

Art. 5(5): 25 ppb & 250 ppb Targeted PFAS

Food-contact packaging cannot be placed on the EU market on or after August 12, 2026 if it contains >= 25 ppb of any single targeted PFAS (non-polymeric) or >= 250 ppb sum of targeted PFAS.

Art. 5(5): 50 ppm Total Fluorine / Polymeric PFAS

Total PFAS including polymeric PFAS in food-contact packaging is capped below 50 ppm (50 mg/kg); if total fluorine exceeds 50 mg F/kg, proof of PFAS vs. non-PFAS fluorine content is required.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why do supplier lab reports cause unit-conversion errors on PFAS vs. heavy metals?
Heavy metal test reports typically report in mg/kg (ppm), whereas targeted PFAS LC-MS/MS reports often report in ug/kg (ppb) or ng/g. Confusing ppm and ppb creates a 1,000x threshold error—which PackProof normalizes deterministically.
How is 'ND' (Not Detected) handled when summing Pb + Cd + Hg + Cr(VI)?
PackProof checks both lower-bound (ND = 0) and conservative upper-bound (ND = Method Detection Limit / LOQ) sums so compliance engineers immediately spot high-LOQ screening reports that could exceed 100 mg/kg.
