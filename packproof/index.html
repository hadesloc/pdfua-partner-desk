    Active Since 12 Aug 2026

    Article 5 Heavy Metals

    Sum of concentration levels of Lead (Pb) + Cadmium (Cd) + Mercury (Hg) + Hexavalent Chromium (Cr VI) ≤ 100 mg/kg in packaging or packaging components.

    Active Since 12 Aug 2026

    Article 5(5) Food-Contact PFAS

    Food-contact packaging may not be placed on the EU market at or above: 25 ppb any single targeted non-polymeric PFAS, 250 ppb sum of targeted non-polymeric PFAS, or 50 ppm (mg/kg) total PFAS (with origin proof required if total fluorine > 50 mg/kg).
