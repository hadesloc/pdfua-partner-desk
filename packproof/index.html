    Primary-source thresholds

    Regulation (EU) 2025/40 (PPWR) reference rules

    Cross-checked against official EUR-Lex texts. Verify secondary acts and national Member State rules for your specific product category.

    Active since 12 Aug 2026

    Article 5 heavy metals

    Combined concentration of Lead (Pb) + Cadmium (Cd) + Mercury (Hg) + Hexavalent Chromium (Cr VI) ≤ 100 mg/kg in packaging or packaging components.

    Active since 12 Aug 2026

    Article 5(5) food-contact PFAS

    Food-contact packaging limits: < 25 ppb any single targeted non-polymeric PFAS, < 250 ppb sum of targeted non-polymeric PFAS, and < 50 ppm (mg/kg) total PFAS.
